T30 city polish: I rewrote web/src/city into plain-TS logic (motion, camera, cityLayout, cityModel, cityLogic) plus a Pixi scene (scene/*) and a React HUD (CityView + city.css). The data contract is unchanged (snapshot/trucks/flow/workers/execution over /ws, POST /api/simulator/trucks). Visually there is now a deterministic 2.5D city with raised blocks, long soft shadows, glowing lane dashes, parks, ponds and a vignette. Trucks have a cab, cargo box, gradient headlight cone, status roof light and a pulse ring when stopped; their paths are smoothed so corners round off and starts/stops ease in. I checked it with the exact ci-web steps run locally (npm ci, lint, 40 Vitest tests including murmur2 vectors, easing, corner turning, trails, camera and model; tsc + vite build), all green. I also ran it with VITE_USE_MOCK=1 against a throwaway local stub server and took headless-Edge screenshots of overview, zoom, hover tooltip, drag-drop ripple and the recovery state.
