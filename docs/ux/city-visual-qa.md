# City visual QA (T102)

Scope: `web/src/city/**` only. Baseline: `docs/ux/visual-qa.md` (city items) and the screenshots
`desktop-03-city-zoomed.png` / `mobile-03-city-zoomed.png`. Every logic change has a unit test in
`web/src/city/__tests__/` (`smoothing`, `stream`, `dispatch`).

## Changed

| # | Item | Before | After / why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Truck motion jitter** | Each `trucks` frame (every 250 ms) was stamped with its *arrival* time. Network and main-thread jitter (±40 ms or more) went straight into the path, so trucks sped up and slowed down from frame to frame. | `SampleClock` (motion.ts) turns arrivals into evenly spaced sample times. Each stamp is the last stamp plus the learned interval, nudged towards the real arrival so it does not drift. A stamp is never in the future and never more than 180 ms behind, and it resyncs after a long gap. Unit test: interval spread is less than half the raw jitter, and steady input gives steady rendered speed. |
| 2 | **Corners** | When the two samples either side of a junction were on different streets, the smoothed curve cut diagonally across the block corner. | `streetCorner()` detects that case on the 200 m grid. `TruckTrack.push` then inserts the junction as an extra sample, with its time interpolated by distance. The existing midpoint/quadratic smoothing then rounds a tight corner on the street. Heading follows the curve tangent. Drop-in glides switch this off. Test: the path stays within 10 m of a street through the turn, and the heading swings east → south. |
| 3 | **Event stream rate** | One global rate, spread round-robin over all trucks, so every lane looked equally busy. | `laneVisualRates()` splits the (sub-linear, capped) visual rate across the 6 lanes in proportion to `flow.byPartition`. Each lane spawns from trucks that really hash to that partition (Kafka murmur2), so a quiet partition really goes quiet. Rejected and late particles use the truck's real lane. Test: proportions are kept, the reduced-motion cap holds, and spawn counts over 10 s at 30/60/144 fps match the rate. |
| 4 | **Engine state in the stream** | The stream looked the same in every state except idle. | `streamStyle(mode)`: **backpressured** is slower and a little dimmer. **Recovering** is muted lavender (matching the pill), slower and fainter. Stopped, failed, idle and disconnected stop spawning. |
| 5 | **Worker killed** | Nothing in the stream showed which partitions had lost their worker. | `stalledLanes(workers)` marks the partitions of a dead worker until a live RUNNING worker owns them again. Particles heading there turn grey and faint and do not light the lane when they arrive. The engine lane gets a muted red outline and "stalled" in place of its rate. Test: kill, still restoring, and recovered cases. |
| 6 | **Disconnected** (visual-qa: "the city keeps animating as if live") | With the WebSocket down the city kept replaying the last rate. | `CityModel.connected` comes from the socket status. The status pill shows "Live link lost · reconnecting" in amber, the stream stops, and the canvas is desaturated and dimmed. |
| 7 | **Truck status colours** | The legend had Driving, Unloading and Stopped. Stopped and Rejected used the same red dot (visual-qa a11y note). | The legend now lists Driving (cyan), Unloading (amber), **Idle** (grey) and Stopped (red alert, with a halo). Trucks are drawn as rounded squares and stream packets as short comets, so Stopped and Rejected differ by shape as well as colour. The worker summary says "worker down" when one is dead. |
| 8 | **Drag-a-truck affordance** | The only hint was "Drag the chip onto a street", shown on click. A failed drop said "Simulator unavailable", whatever the cause. Drops on the engine panel were silently clamped to the city edge. | The chip has a grip handle and a title, and a persistent "· drag truck onto map" hint sits next to the events/s. While dragging: a top banner reads "Release to add a truck here", or "Outside the city · drop on a street" with a red border and a red ✕ reticle. A drop shows a cyan ripple and "Dispatching truck…", then "truck-009 dispatched" (green) or an error (red, with "!", shown for 4.2 s and a red ripple). The error is the contract-04 `message`, `HTTP <status>`, or "Simulator unreachable". **Click / Enter** adds a truck at the depot, so keyboard users can add one too. Notices are `role=status aria-live=polite`. The logic lives in `dispatch.ts`. Test: inside, edge and outside drops, success, error body, HTTP error and network error. |
| 9 | **Truck id labels** (visual-qa Low: about 6 px, unreadable) | Always drawn. | Drawn only when they are at least 9 px tall on screen (zoomed in), or on hover. |
| 10 | **Packets over rooftops when zoomed** (visual-qa Low) | Full strength at every zoom. | `zoomFade()` keeps them at full strength up to 1.5× zoom and fades them to 35% by 3× zoom. |
| 11 | **Engine column text** (visual-qa Medium: about 7 px) | Lane rates at 23 world-px, the state caption at 23, and worker labels at 19. | Raised to 32, 28 and 24 world-px, and rate text contrast increased. Still small at full-city zoom; see "Left". |
| 12 | **Controls hint** (visual-qa Medium: about 10 px grey, < 3:1, overlaps the map) | Bare text at 45% opacity. | A glass pill with #a9c2d1 text at 11.5 px (> 4.5:1 on navy). It shows only while the pointer is over the city and is hidden below 1080 px. |
| 13 | **Mobile pill overlap** (visual-qa mobile-04b) | "Recovering from checkpoint #133" covered the title card at 390 px. | Below 520 px the status pill sits under the title card. |
| 14 | **60 fps / allocations** | Every frame built a string key from all the stops (`map().join()`) and a new `sources` array of new objects. `EngineCore` called `Array.from`, spread `Math.max`, `slice` and a `forEach` closure every frame. Each spawn encoded the truck id to compute its partition. | Stops are compared by identity. Sources are the persistent `RenderedTruck` objects, kept in reused per-lane arrays inside one reused `FlowFrame`. The partition is computed once per truck. Engine rates use a fixed array and plain loops. The particle pool is unchanged (220 cap, 40 with reduced motion). |
| 15 | **prefers-reduced-motion** | The canvas already capped particles and froze pulses. | Drop ripples now draw a single ring. The new CSS transitions and animations (hint, notices, drop banner, offline filter) are disabled under reduce. |

## Checked
- `npm run lint` (0 errors), `npm run test` (19 files, 104 tests, 30 of them new in `__tests__/smoothing|stream|dispatch`), and `npm run build` all pass locally (Node 22).
- No existing test was changed or removed. The original `TruckTrack` behaviour (hold at the newest midpoint when data is late, teleport reset, out-of-order samples ignored) is unchanged and still tested.
